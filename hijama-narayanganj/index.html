Important Medical Note

Cupping is a complementary therapy and should not be presented as a cure for serious medical conditions.

Evidence for cupping varies by condition. If you have a medical condition, take blood-thinning medicine, have a bleeding disorder, significant anemia, are pregnant, or have another health concern, discuss whether cupping is appropriate with a qualified healthcare professional before treatment.

Safety First

Hijama Safety & Hygiene

Wet cupping involves skin piercing and blood exposure, so appropriate infection-control practices are essential.

🧤 Hygiene

Appropriate hand hygiene, gloves, clean treatment surfaces and safe handling of equipment should be maintained throughout the session.

🧪 Equipment

Blood-contact equipment should be appropriately disposable or processed according to proper infection-control procedures.

🩹 Possible Effects

  • Temporary circular marks
  • Mild soreness
  • Skin irritation
  • Bleeding
  • Infection risk

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Hijama in Narayanganj

What is Hijama?
Hijama commonly refers to wet cupping, in which suction is followed by controlled superficial skin piercing. Dry cupping uses suction without skin piercing.
Do you provide Hijama in Narayanganj?
Hijama Dhaka provides appointment-based Hijama and cupping services for clients in Narayanganj, subject to practitioner availability and location confirmation.
Is Hijama safe?
Cupping can have risks and side effects, including temporary skin marks, soreness, burns, bleeding and infection. Appropriate hygiene and infection-control practices are important.
Does Hijama cure diseases?
Hijama should not be promoted as a cure for serious diseases. It is a complementary therapy, and evidence varies depending on the condition. Appropriate medical diagnosis and treatment should not be replaced.
Can I request a female Hijama practitioner?
Yes. You may request a female practitioner when booking. Availability depends on the practitioner schedule and service location.
What equipment is used?
The service uses appropriate cups and treatment materials, with disposable blood-contact items where applicable. Safe handling and infection-control procedures are important for wet cupping.
How long does a Hijama session take?
Session length can vary depending on the type of cupping, number of treatment areas and individual circumstances. The practitioner can provide an estimated duration during booking.
Can people taking blood thinners have Hijama?
People taking medicines that affect blood clotting should discuss whether wet cupping is appropriate with a qualified healthcare professional before treatment.
Can pregnant women have Hijama?
Pregnancy requires individual medical consideration. Anyone who is pregnant should discuss whether cupping is appropriate with an appropriate healthcare professional before treatment.
