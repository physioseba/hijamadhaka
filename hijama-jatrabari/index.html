Important medical safety information

Hijama is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment of a serious medical condition. Wet cupping involves skin injury and can cause bleeding, infection, bruising or scarring. People with bleeding disorders, significant anemia, active skin infection, or those taking blood-thinning medicines should obtain appropriate medical advice before wet cupping.

If you have severe or unexplained symptoms, seek medical care rather than relying on cupping.

Who Should Be Careful?

Before Booking Wet Hijama

A short health screening is important before any procedure that breaks the skin.

  • Taking anticoagulant or antiplatelet medicines
  • Known bleeding or clotting disorders
  • Significant anemia or a history of fainting with procedures
  • Active skin infection, open wound or severe dermatitis at the treatment site
  • Pregnancy or other medical circumstances requiring clinician advice

What to tell the practitioner

Tell the practitioner about your current medicines, allergies, chronic conditions, pregnancy status where relevant, previous reactions to procedures, and the exact area you want treated.

Do not stop prescribed medicine just to receive Hijama. Discuss medication concerns with the prescribing clinician.
